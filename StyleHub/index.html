How to Measure

Use a soft measuring tape for best results. Measurements are in centimetres.

📐
Chest Measurement
Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
📏
Waist Measurement
Measure around your natural waistline, the narrowest part of your torso.
💡
Between Two Sizes?
We always recommend sizing up for a more comfortable, relaxed fit — especially for dresses and jackets.
