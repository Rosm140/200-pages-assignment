Frequently Asked Questions

Is HomeQuest free to use as a buyer? +
Yes! Browsing properties, searching, and contacting agents is completely free for buyers. You only need a paid plan if you want to list properties or access premium seller features.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Absolutely. You can cancel your Pro or Enterprise subscription at any time from your account dashboard. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.
Does HomeQuest charge commission on property sales? +
Our platform subscription pricing is separate from any commission charged by individual agents. Agent commissions are negotiated directly and vary by property type and value.
