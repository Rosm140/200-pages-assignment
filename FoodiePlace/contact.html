Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery timings?+
We deliver 7 days a week from 11:00 AM to 10:30 PM. Average delivery time is 30–45 minutes depending on your location and order volume.
Is there a minimum order for delivery?+
There is no minimum order amount. However, orders below ₹500 attract a delivery fee of ₹49. Orders above ₹500 get FREE delivery.
Can I customise my order?+
Absolutely! You can add special requests like No Onion, Less Spicy, Gluten-Free etc. in the Order Special Requests section. For complex customisations, call us directly.
How do I cancel or modify an order?+
Orders can be cancelled within 2 minutes of placing. After that, call us at +91 98765 43210 immediately and we'll do our best to accommodate changes.
Do you accept large group reservations?+
Yes! We can accommodate groups up to 50 people. For groups of 10+, please contact us directly at reservations@foodieplace.in to discuss a customised menu and seating arrangement.
Are there vegetarian/vegan options?+
Yes! Over 40% of our menu is vegetarian and we have dedicated vegan options as well. All dishes are clearly labelled. Ask our staff for the allergen menu.
