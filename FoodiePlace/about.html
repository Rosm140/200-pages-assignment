Our Story

Born from a Grandmother's Kitchen

FoodiePlace was born from a simple belief: great food has the power to bring people together, create memories and nourish the soul. Our founder, Arjun Mehta, grew up watching his grandmother transform humble ingredients into extraordinary meals in her small kitchen in Hyderabad.

After years of training under renowned chefs across Italy and France, Arjun returned home with a vision — to create a restaurant that blended classical European techniques with the warmth and generosity of Indian hospitality.

Today, FoodiePlace stands as a testament to that vision: a place where every dish tells a story, every guest is treated like family, and every meal is an experience worth remembering.
