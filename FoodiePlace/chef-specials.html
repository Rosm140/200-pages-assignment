Meet the Artist

Chef Marco Rossi

Executive Head Chef · FoodiePlace

Born in Naples, Italy, Chef Marco discovered his love for food at age seven in his grandmother's kitchen. After training at the prestigious Le Cordon Bleu in Paris and working in 3-Michelin-star restaurants across Europe, he brought his mastery of Italian-European fusion cuisine to Hyderabad.

His philosophy is simple: let the ingredients speak. Every dish on the specials menu is a reflection of the season's finest produce, cooked with techniques refined over 20 years of culinary excellence.
