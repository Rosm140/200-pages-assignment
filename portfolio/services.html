Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

How long does a typical project take?+
It depends on scope. A simple landing page takes 3–5 days, a full web app typically takes 3–6 weeks. I'll give you a detailed timeline in the proposal before we begin.
Do you work with international clients?+
Absolutely! I work with clients worldwide. I'm flexible with time zones and accept international payments via Wise, PayPal, and Stripe. Contracts available in English.
What's your payment structure?+
I typically work with a 50% upfront deposit and 50% on delivery. For larger projects, I use milestone-based payments. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.
Will I own the code after delivery?+
Yes — you get full ownership of all source code once final payment is received. I also provide a private GitHub repository, full documentation, and a deployment handoff guide.
Do you offer post-launch support?+
Every project includes 30 days of free post-launch support for bug fixes. Beyond that, you can choose one of my monthly retainer plans or purchase ad-hoc support hours.
Can you work with my existing team?+
Yes! I'm experienced at collaborating with in-house teams, designers, and other developers. I follow standard Git workflows, write documentation, and adapt to your existing tech stack where possible.
